# ROAMA — mini-site

Static website of the ROAMA web studio: home, privacy policy and terms of use, in Ukrainian (root) and English (`en/`).
It is the homepage and the policy pages of our internal app «ROAMA Publisher».

- Plain HTML and CSS, no JavaScript, no cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests (fonts are self-hosted).
- Live at https://roama-studio.github.io/ (GitHub Pages of the ROAMA account, repo `roama-studio/roama-studio.github.io`).
  All page links are relative; only `404.html` uses root paths, because GitHub serves it at any missing address.
- Fonts: Unbounded and Onest, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/OFL-*.txt`), subset to Latin + Cyrillic.
- The policy and terms texts were written for ROAMA and reviewed by the owner of ROAMA; they are not legal advice and
  were not reviewed by a lawyer.
