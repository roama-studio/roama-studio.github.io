Privacy policy

Updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what data ROAMA processes, on this website and in our app ROAMA Publisher. Send questions and requests to roama.studio@gmail.com.

This website

  • The website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or forms. Its fonts are served by the site itself, so your browser does not contact any other server.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub may process technical data of visitors (such as the IP address) to run and protect the site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The ROAMA Publisher app

ROAMA Publisher is our internal tool. Only ROAMA uses it, to publish our own videos and texts to ROAMA's own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube and TikTok, and to see the statistics of our own posts. The app is not offered to other people and does not work with anyone else's accounts.

What data it receives

  • The identifiers and names of our own accounts, page and channel.
  • Permission to publish videos and texts on behalf of these accounts; the status and links of what was published.
  • Statistics of our posts: views, likes, number of comments.
  • Comments and messages that people choose to send to our accounts, with their public username, only so that we can reply.

How we use and store it

  • The data is stored on ROAMA's computer. Access tokens are kept in a local configuration file and are never published.
  • We do not sell the data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising.
  • To draft replies we may use third-party service providers (including AI services) that process data only on our behalf. Replies to comments may be written and sent automatically by our AI assistant; we can switch this off at any time and answer ourselves.
  • We delete access tokens as soon as access is revoked. We keep the rest while ROAMA's accounts are active, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Data received from the YouTube API (statistics, comments) is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. After access is revoked we delete YouTube data within 7 days if it was revoked in the app, and within 30 days if it was revoked on Google's page.

Google and YouTube data

For YouTube the app uses the YouTube API Services. By using them we follow the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. ROAMA Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access and delete data

  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions. We can also revoke the access ourselves with one command of the app: it cancels the permission at Google and deletes our access token at once.
  • Facebook and Instagram: your account settings, under "Business integrations" or "Apps and websites".
  • TikTok: "Settings and privacy" → "Security" → "Manage app permissions".
  • To have us delete your data (for example, your comment in our records), write to roama.studio@gmail.com. We delete it within 7 days.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, object to its processing, and complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
